Hi there!

I am Davide, and I am a lecturer and researcher at the
[MeDiTech Institute](https://www.supsi.ch/meditech) of
the [University of Applied Sciences and Arts of Southern Switzerland (SUPSI)](https://www.supsi.ch).
My current research interests are:

- collection, harmonization, and analysis of the data collected during normal life through wearable devices
- personalized modeling
- machine learning applied to longitudinal data


Prior to joining SUPSI, I was a research scientist and project manager at [PRIMA Lab SA](https://primalabsa.ch/en/), now part of [Sentinel Diagnostics](https://www.sentineldiagnostics.com/it/).
I hold a PhD in Bioengineering from Politecnico di Milano, with a thesis focused on the diagnosis of lung cancer through exhaled breath analysis, carried out in collaboration with the European Institute of Oncology (IEO), in Milan.
I hold a Bachelor of Science in Biomedical Engineering (Politecnico di Milano), a Master of Science in Biomedical Engineering (Politecnico di Milano), and in Bioengineering (University of Illinois at Chicago).

In my [curriculum vitae](../cv) you can find more about my experience and education.
Everything on this page consists only of my own thoughts and is not in any 
way connected to my current affiliation. Feel free to get in touch by clicking 
at the icons below!
